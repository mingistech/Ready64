Ready64 bundled fonts
=====================

Current typefaces (selectable in Ready64 > Settings…):

- Commodore-64-v6.3.TTF          → Commodore 64
- Commodore Angled v1.2.ttf     → Commodore 64 Angled
- Commodore Rounded v1.2.ttf    → Commodore 64 Rounded

These are registered at launch and chosen via Settings.
Font choice affects display only and is never saved into .txt documents.

Do not commit or redistribute a font unless its license permits it.
